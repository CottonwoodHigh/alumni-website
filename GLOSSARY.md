# Cottonwood High alumni website

The alumni website for Cottonwood High School.
It gives each graduating class a page for contacts, reunions, media, and memorials.

## Language

**Class**:
All students who graduated from Cottonwood High School in the same year.
Its name is its graduation year, as in "Class of 1972".
_Avoid_: course, cohort, roster, year group

**Class page**:
The one page on this site for a Class, at `/classes/YYYY/`.
_Avoid_: class homepage (a Class often has its own site elsewhere)
