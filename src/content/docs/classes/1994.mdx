## Reunions

### 2024 (30-year)

Colts '94!
We held our 30-year reunion over the weekend of 20–21 Sep 2024, with the main dinner event on Saturday.
We invited everyone, not just the cool kids, because let's be honest, nobody was that cool.

- **Date:** Friday and Saturday, 20–21 Sep 2024
- **Main dinner event:** Saturday, 21 Sep 2024
