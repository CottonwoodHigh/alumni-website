---
title: 'Booking Cottonwood'
description: Information on how to book Cottonwood as a venue.
---

For Cottonwood reunions, Granite School District doesn't charge for the venue itself, only for the personnel.
Specifically, they require an administrator and a custodian to be present starting with the set-up time through the take-down.
They also charge 3–4 hours for the custodian to clean up after the event.
The hourly rate for each individual is $50 (at time of writing, subject to change).

To request a date, use [ML Schedules](https://ut1.mlschedules.com/ReportCalendar.aspx) for Granite School District to submit rental requests.
Note that you must submit all requests at least 14 days in advance!

When you set up an account to make a request, it will require liability insurance before letting you proceed.
Any insurance provider can supply liability insurance.
Granite School District specifically requires the following.

{/* "Additional Insured", "Primary", and "Aggregate" are the terms Granite
     School District and the insurer expect, so they stay as written. */}
{/* vale write-good.TooWordy = NO */}

1. Granite School District must appear as "Additional Insured" on a primary/non-contributory basis.
2. Limits must be at least $1,000,000 Primary per occurrence and $2,000,000 Aggregate.
3. Email a copy of the Certificate of Liability Insurance to [`rentals@graniteschools.org`](mailto:rentals@graniteschools.org).

{/* vale write-good.TooWordy = YES */}

See Granite School District's [Facility Rental Page](https://www.graniteschools.org/facilityrentals/) for the most up-to-date information.
